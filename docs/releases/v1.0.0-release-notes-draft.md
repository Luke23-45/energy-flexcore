## 7. Wording discipline — what this release does NOT promise

Quoted verbatim from `AGENTS.md` §3 (rules 2–3):

> 2. **No safety or certification claims.** FlexCore is not certified,
> not a safety case, and not permission for live grid control. Never
> write text that implies otherwise.
> 3. **No protection-grade claims.** FlexCore is a supervisory
> orchestration layer. It does not perform primary frequency response,
> synthetic inertia, relay protection, inverter inner-loop control, or
> sub-cycle control.

Quoted verbatim from the report §0.3 (what is deliberately not
promised) — the items relevant to this release:

> - hard real-time or protection-grade control;
> - primary frequency response or synthetic inertia control;
> - autonomous grid switching or protection-system operation;
> - universal AC-OPF global optimality;
> - universal market qualification or FERC Order 2222 compliance;
> - certified OpenADR, IEEE 2030.5, IEC 61850, SunSpec, or OCPP
>   conformance merely by implementing an adapter;
> - safe field dispatch without site-specific integration,
>   commissioning, cyber review, and hardware-in-the-loop validation;
>   or
> - that GNN or MARL outputs are safe by construction.

These are separate qualification and research tracks. Solver-class
discipline also holds verbatim in substance: HiGHS is LP/QP/MIP, not
SOCP; Clarabel is convex conic, not MISOCP; AC power flow is not
AC-OPF; IPOPT is local, not a global-optimality guarantee (AGENTS.md
§3 rule 4).
